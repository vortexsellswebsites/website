QUESTIONS.

What kind of items do you sell?
We sell a mix of clothing, household items, shoes, random finds, seasonal items, and other things that change from sale to sale.
Can I buy items online?
Currently, our items are available during our scheduled yard sales.
Do you offer delivery?
Delivery is not currently offered. Please visit the sale in person to check out the available items.
How can I check what's in stock?
Inventory changes frequently. Check the website and Facebook page for updates before the next sale.
Are items tested or cleaned?
Items are looked over before being placed out for sale. Condition can vary, so please inspect items before purchasing.
Do you accept donations or trades?
Please contact us before bringing items for donation or trade.
